### 6.2 Information Collected Automatically

### 6.3 Information from Third Parties

The information available depends on the third party, your settings with that third party, and the permissions granted to the integration.

### 6.4 Bot-Specific Information Collected

The Bot collects the following, each for the stated purpose:

- **Support tickets.** When you use `/create-ticket`, `/create-ticket-from-message`, or a `/support-ticket-*` command, the ticket body, the ticket category, and **the Discord user ID of the person who opened it** are transmitted to the Freshdesk support portal, and the Bot records the resulting ticket number. *Purpose:* so the support team can answer you. **Audit logging:** the Bot's own logs record *who* initiated a ticket action by Discord ID; they do **not** record the ticket body, your submitted name, your email address, your subject line, or your message text.
- **Moderation records.** The Bot records the Discord user ID of the affected member and of the acting moderator, the action taken, the channel, any stated reason, and — where a content filter matched — a short excerpt of the triggering message. *Purpose:* to apply and justify moderation decisions and to review prior warnings.
- **Honeypot channels.** Administrators may designate channels as honeypots — channels no user should need to post in. Posting in an enabled honeypot may trigger an automated action against that account, which is logged. Honeypots are inactive until an administrator explicitly creates and enables one; no channel is a honeypot by default.
- **Member activity summaries.** Your Discord user ID, username, display name, first and last message timestamps, total message count, and number of active days. *Purpose:* to display aggregate activity trends. **This is counting only — the Bot does not read or store the text of your messages for this feature.**
- **Translated and moderated message content.** Where a feature requires message content to operate, that content is stored **encrypted at rest** using a symmetric cipher, with the key held separately from the data on the Server. Encrypted values are marked with an `enc:` prefix. *Purpose:* only to operate the feature.
- **Web GUI accounts.** An email address and a **one-way hashed password** — the original password is never stored and cannot be recovered (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) — together with an administrator flag and a password-age record used to enforce periodic credential rotation. *Purpose:* to control who may access the Web GUI.
- **Server configuration.** Per-server settings are stored for features an administrator enables, including channel and role definitions, per-command permission settings, monitoring subscriptions, honeypot and logging configuration, and reaction roles. *Purpose:* to remember how the Bot should behave on that Server.
- **Service credentials.** Credentials for the Bot's own integrations — for example a Freshdesk API key, an uptime monitoring API key, a Reddit client secret, or a Discourse API key — are stored in a configuration file on the Server with owner-only permissions. **Secret values are never written to logs.** Where a log line must confirm a credential is present, it records only the word `configured` or `missing`, never the value.

### 6.5 Information We Do Not Collect

The Bot does **not** collect your email address from Discord or send you email; does not read or store the text of your messages except as expressly described above, and never for analytics or profiling; does not use tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, browser fingerprinting, or third-party analytics; does not build advertising or behavioural profiles; does not sell, rent, trade, license, or otherwise monetize any information it holds; and does not operate on your device or browser — it executes only on the Server it is hosted on.
